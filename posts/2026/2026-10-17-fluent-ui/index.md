# Fluent UI - user experience, design language and functional programming

My father was man with golden hands, generation of people who could do everthing from buld a house to being electronics enhusiast. From young age he was inspiring me doing high quality wood works. My father was teaching me that craft becomes valuable when to apply attension to details. I remember when opened a book about woodworks and the first chapter was about how to keep table clean and instruments sharp. Later this wisdom resonates with [Steve Jobs](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/11084145-steve-jobs) childhood experience written by Walter Isaacson.


Over decade ago, I was enthusiastically learning [Windows User Experience Itenraction Guildelines (Windows 7, Vista)](./assets/windows_user_experience_interaction_guidelines_windows_7_vista.pdf)
 to get Windows Logo certification for software that we were developing. I remember when we were creating short memos for those who didn't have patience to read 900 pages of guidelines.

Windows, layout, text, controls, links, tooltips, progress bars, prompts, notifications, keyboard and mouse, dialog boxes, wizards, error and warnings messages, icons, help - things were modernized but Design Language and User Experience patterns are still serving the same goal.
